G wish и if only
T wish + Past Simple — сожаление о настоящем: I wish I had more time. wish + Past Perfect — сожаление о прошлом: I wish I had hired her. wish + would — раздражение от чужого поведения или желание, чтобы кто-то изменился: I wish he would reply. If only — то же самое, только эмоциональнее. В формальной речи после wish говорят were: I wish I were there.

X Одно письмо — два стиля | Как выбрать тон
P Formal version: "Dear Mr. Chen, I hope this email finds you well. With reference to our meeting on Tuesday, please find attached the revised proposal. We would be grateful if you could review it at your earliest convenience. Should you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me."
P Informal version: "Hi Mike, thanks for Tuesday! Here's the new proposal. Could you have a look when you get a chance? Ping me if anything's unclear. Cheers, Alex"
P Both messages say exactly the same thing, but they create very different impressions. The first one is safe for a new client, a government office or a large corporation. The second one works well with a partner you already know, especially in startups and creative industries.
P A good rule of thumb: mirror the other person. If they write "Dear", reply with "Dear". If they write "Hey", you can relax a bit. And when in doubt, it's better to be a little too formal than a little too casual.
